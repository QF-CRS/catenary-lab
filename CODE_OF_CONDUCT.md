# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing
private information without permission, and other conduct that would be
unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer through the
contact method listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed and
handled in confidence.
